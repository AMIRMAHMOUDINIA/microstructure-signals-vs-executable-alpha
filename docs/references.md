# References

1. Cont, R., Kukanov, A., & Stoikov, S. **The Price Impact of Order Book Events.** The study develops order-flow imbalance as a short-horizon price-impact variable and reports a robust approximately linear relationship between OFI and price changes.

2. Cont, R., Cucuringu, M., & Zhang, C. **Cross-Impact of Order Flow Imbalance in Equity Markets.** Extends OFI analysis across book levels and examines lagged forecasting effects.

3. Binance Public Data. Official downloadable daily/monthly market-data archives for spot and futures, including USD-M futures klines and trade files. Kline fields include open/high/low/close, volume, quote volume, trade count, taker-buy base volume and taker-buy quote volume. Each archive has an accompanying SHA-256 `.CHECKSUM` file.

## Terminology note

The project uses **trade-flow imbalance (TFI)** for the kline-derived aggressive buyer/seller signal. It does not label this variable OFI because full OFI also includes limit-order additions and cancellations.
