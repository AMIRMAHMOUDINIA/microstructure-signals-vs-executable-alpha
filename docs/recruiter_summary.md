# Recruiter Summary

## Project in one paragraph

Built a reproducible BTCUSDT market-microstructure research pipeline that separated **statistical significance, out-of-sample prediction, and economic viability**. Static L1 book imbalance showed a stable positive 10-minute return relationship and a logistic baseline retained weak untouched-test ranking power (AUC 0.5103), but a frozen cost-aware execution study failed its independent economic gate. A second study added aggressive taker-flow features, improving pre-OOS AUC by +0.0042 to +0.0060 while rejecting the original continuation hypothesis: aggressive flow consistently behaved as a reversal signal. A fresh 2025 study replicated the negative TFI-return relationship across all six pre-gate months, but the signal was not robustly incremental to simple price reversal, so the final OOS period remained closed.

## What this demonstrates

- hypothesis-driven research rather than backtest-first optimization;
- market-microstructure feature engineering;
- leakage-aware chronological validation;
- HAC/Newey-West inference for overlapping labels;
- FDR, block bootstrap and non-overlapping robustness checks;
- explicit test-set and gate discipline;
- cost-aware execution modelling;
- willingness to preserve rejected hypotheses and null economic results;
- Python engineering with automated tests and reproducible data pipelines.

## Interview headline

**"I found statistically reproducible microstructure predictability, then showed why it was not sufficient to claim executable alpha."**
