# Microstructure Signals vs Executable Alpha

## Abstract

This project studies short-horizon BTCUSDT perpetual-futures predictability through three sequential experiments. Study I finds a stable positive relationship between static top-of-book imbalance and future 10-minute mid-price returns; a frozen logistic model retains weak untouched-test ranking power (AUC 0.5103). A conservative execution study then fails its independent economic confirmation gate, showing that statistical predictability is not sufficient for executable alpha. Study II adds aggressive taker trade flow and improves pre-OOS AUC, but rejects the pre-registered continuation hypothesis: trade-flow imbalance is consistently associated with reversal. Study III tests that unexpected reversal pattern on fresh 2025 data and replicates it across every Jan-Jun month. The stronger claim that flow adds robust information beyond simple price mean reversion does not pass its gate, so the final Jul-Dec 2025 OOS remains closed.

The main result is methodological as much as empirical: **predictability, generalization and monetizability are distinct claims and require distinct evidence.**
